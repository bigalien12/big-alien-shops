Request Account Deletion

Customers can request deletion of their BIG ALIEN account and associated personal data.

How to request deletion

Contact our customer service using the customer service contact details on our official website. Include the email address registered to your account and request account deletion.

What happens next?

We will verify your request and delete your account and associated personal data, except information we are legally required to retain. We will explain any applicable retention period when responding to your request.

You do not need to keep the app installed to request deletion.
