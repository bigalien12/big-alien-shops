Request Account Deletion

Customers can request deletion of their BIG ALIEN account and associated personal data.

How to request deletion

To request deletion of your BIG ALIEN account and associated personal data, contact our customer service through our official WhatsApp support channel. Include the email address registered to your account and write “Account Deletion Request”. We will verify your identity and process your request, except for information we are legally required to retain. Include the email address registered to your account and request account deletion.

What happens next?

We will verify your request and delete your account and associated personal data, except information we are legally required to retain. We will explain any applicable retention period when responding to your request.

You do not need to keep the app installed to request deletion.
