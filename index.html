Frequently asked questions

How do I track my order?

Open Account → Orders to view your order and any shipment updates added by BIG ALIEN. If you need more help, contact us with your order ID.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery timing depends on the destination and order. Check your order updates for the latest progress, or contact support with your order ID for an update.

How can I request a return or refund?

Contact BIG ALIEN Support with your order ID and explain the issue. Our team will review your request and confirm the available next steps.

What should I do if my payment is not confirmed?

Check your order status first. If it still shows unpaid after you have made a transfer, contact support and share your order ID and payment evidence. Please do not send payment details such as your PIN or password.

How do I contact customer service?

Use the WhatsApp or phone buttons below. Include your order ID when asking about an existing order so we can assist you faster.
